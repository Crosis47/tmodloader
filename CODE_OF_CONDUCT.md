# Code of Conduct

## Our community

We want this project to be a welcoming place to ask questions, report problems,
share ideas, and contribute. Everyone is expected to treat others with respect,
regardless of their background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and patient, especially with people who are learning.
- Give specific, constructive feedback about the work. Disagree with ideas
  without attacking the person presenting them.
- Respect different perspectives and accept corrections in good faith.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project and respect maintainers' time.
- Respect people's privacy and boundaries. Ask before sharing personal details
  or contacting someone outside the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, or discriminatory abuse.
- Insults, personal attacks, or deliberate attempts to provoke or disrupt others.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or sexualized content.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission, including
  contact details, player addresses, credentials, or private conversations.
- Repeated unwanted contact, retaliation against someone who reports a concern,
  or attempts to evade moderation restrictions.
- Spam or repeated disruption of issues, pull requests, or other project spaces.

## Scope

This policy applies to this repository's issues, pull requests, discussions,
wiki, and other project-managed community spaces. It also applies when someone
is officially representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting a concern

Report concerns to the project maintainer, [Crosis47](https://github.com/Crosis47).
If you already have an agreed private contact channel, use it. Otherwise, open
a minimal [issue](https://github.com/Crosis47/tmodloader/issues/new) asking the
maintainer to arrange a private conversation. Do not include incident details,
names of people involved, screenshots, or other sensitive information in that
public request. Share the report only after a private channel is established.

In the private report, include what happened, relevant links or evidence, and
any immediate concerns. Maintainers will handle reports as privately as
practical and limit sharing to what is needed to assess and address the issue.
Reports will be reviewed as maintainer availability permits; this project does
not provide an emergency response service.

For GitHub account abuse, or if your concern involves the project maintainer,
you can use [GitHub's abuse reporting process](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse).
Report software vulnerabilities using [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Moderation

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock discussions, reject contributions,
or restrict participation to protect the community. Responses will consider
the severity, impact, and pattern of the behavior. They may include a request
to change behavior, a warning, a temporary restriction, or a permanent ban.
Serious threats or harassment may result in an immediate ban.

When practical, maintainers will explain moderation decisions privately.
Someone affected by a decision may request reconsideration through the private
channel established with the maintainer and provide relevant context. Respect
any participation restrictions while that request is considered.
